Unit 1

Structural Isomers vs. Chiral Isomers

Look at how the atoms are connected first, then check if the 3D molecule has "left- vs. right-handedness."

Structural: Linkage Isomers

Different Atom Connected

Bonding changes: The same NO2− ligand binds via Nitrogen or Oxygen.

Structural: Ionization Isomers

Inside vs. Outside Sphere

Partner swap: A ligand inside the metal cage swaps places with a free counter-ion outside.

Chiral Isomers (Enantiomers)

Same Bonds, Mirror Hands

Non-superimposable mirror images: Identical connections and angles, but opposite 3D twist—like your left and right hands.

Unit 2

Geometric Angles & The Mirror Test

How to tell if a stereoisomer is geometric (cis/trans, fac/mer) or chiral.

cis (90°) vs. trans (180°)

Geometric Isomers

Two identical ligands sit either next to each other (90°) or across the metal center (180°).

fac (Facial) vs. mer (Meridional)

For [Ma₃b₃] Complexes

Three identical ligands either form a 90° triangle on one face (fac) or a flat T-shaped belt (mer).

The Golden Rule for Chirality

Internal Mirror Plane Test

If you can slice the molecule into two identical reflection halves, it is Achiral. Twisted clamp rings (like cis-[M(AA)2b2]) block mirror planes!

Self-Check

4-Question Concept Check

Click an option to check your answer immediately.

1. Which complex is chiral (cannot be superimposed on its mirror image)?
Correct: In cis-[Co(en)2Cl2]+, the two bidentate (en) rings create a twisted propeller with no internal mirror plane.
2. How are [Co(NH3)5(NO2)]2+ (N-bound) and [Co(NH3)5(ONO)]2+ (O-bound) related?
Correct: The ligand connects to the metal using a different atom (N vs. O), making them structural linkage isomers.
3. Counting both geometric and chiral forms, how many stereoisomers exist for [Co(en)2Cl2]+?
Correct: The trans isomer is symmetric (1 form), while the cis isomer is chiral and has left- and right-handed forms (2 forms). Total = 3.
4. Are fac-[Ma3b3] and mer-[Ma3b3] chiral?
Correct: Both fac and mer can be sliced into identical halves by an internal mirror plane, so neither is chiral.
